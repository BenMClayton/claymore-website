Express Wash

Offers a quick and efficient cleaning service for your vehicle. This package includes cleaning the tire faces and rims to remove dirt and brake dust, a snow foam pre-wash that loosens and lifts dirt from the vehicle’s surface, and a shampoo wash combined with wax to leave your car sparkling and protected. Additionally, both exterior and interior windows are cleaned to ensure clear visibility. This service is ideal for those who want a fast, effective car wash with attention to detail.

  • TIRE FACE & RIMS
  • SNOW FOAM PRE WASH
  • SHAMPOO WASH & WAX
  • EXTERIOR WINDOWS
  • INTERIOR WINDOWS

Exterior Valet

Offers a detailed and thorough wash for the exterior of your vehicle. This package includes a comprehensive shampoo wash & snow foam prewash for the entire vehicle, cleaning every nook and cranny. Brake dust will be removed from the entire rims, to make them shine again. A ceramic protective coating (~3 month protection) is applied to the paint work of the vehicle, as well as a plastic coating to the trims to bring back colour and provide protection. Exterior and Interior glass will be deep cleaned, ensuring full visibility

  • TIRE FACE & RIMS DEEP CLEAN
  • SNOW FOAM PRE WASH
  • SHAMPOO WASH
  • DOOR SHUTS & BOOT SHUTS
  • CERAMIC COATING
  • EXTERIOR WINDOWS
  • INTERIOR WINDOWS
  • EXTERIOR TRIM

Interior Valet

Offers a deep clean of the interior of the vehicle, hoovering all surfaces, carpets and mats. Wiping down the dashboard, plastic trim and seats (depending on the material)
